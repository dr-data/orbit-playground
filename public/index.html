Show the math
  • Gravity: F = GMm / r² toward Earth’s center
  • Circular speed: v = √(GM / r)
  • Escape speed: v = √(2GM / r)
  • Motion energy: KE = ½ m v²
  • Height energy: PE = −GMm / r
  • Total: TE = KE + PE. Negative means bound to Earth.

Experiment

Why PE and total energy are negative

  1. We set PE = 0 at infinity. That is the top of the hill. Closer to Earth is downhill, so PE = −GMm/r is less than zero.
  2. Kinetic energy is never negative. Speed always adds a positive ½mv².
  3. On a circular orbit, KE = −PE/2. So total energy is PE/2 — still negative. The satellite does not have enough energy to climb all the way out of the well.
  4. Total energy is the verdict. TE < 0 bound (ellipse or circle). TE = 0 just escapes. TE > 0 flies away with leftover speed.

A 30-second tour

  1. Orbit is falling and missing. Gravity always pulls toward Earth’s center. If you throw sideways fast enough, the ground curves away as fast as you fall.
  2. Speed decides the shape. Too slow → crash. Circular speed → circle. A bit fast → oval. Escape speed → leave Earth. LEO is a low circle (~400 km). GEO is a high circle (~35,786 km) that matches Earth’s day.
  3. Energy is the budget. Motion energy is gold. Height energy is purple and negative (closer = more trapped). If total energy is negative, Earth still holds it.
  4. Zoom. Use − / + / Max on the Earth view and on the energy graph if a curve is chopped off. Max shows Earth, the satellite, LEO, and GEO together.
  5. Energy Lab lets you slide height and speed and see why PE and total energy must be negative for a bound orbit.
