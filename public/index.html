Show the math
  • Gravity: F = GMm / r² toward Earth’s center
  • Circular speed: v = √(GM / r)
  • Escape speed: v = √(2GM / r)
  • Motion energy: KE = ½ m v²
  • Height energy: PE = −GMm / r
  • Total: TE = KE + PE. Negative means bound to Earth.

A 30-second tour

  1. Orbit is falling and missing. Gravity always pulls toward Earth’s center. If you throw sideways fast enough, the ground curves away as fast as you fall.
  2. Speed decides the shape. Too slow → crash. Just right → circle. A bit fast → oval. Very fast → escape.
  3. Energy is the budget. Motion energy is gold. Height energy is blue and negative (closer = more trapped). If total energy is negative, Earth still holds it.
  4. Try Boost − on a circular orbit. It does not drop into a smaller circle. It becomes an oval. That surprise is the whole lesson.
